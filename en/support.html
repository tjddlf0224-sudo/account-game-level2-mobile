About the App

Ledger Arcade is an accounting learning app. It helps you pick up key ideas such as journal entries, accounts, and financial statements through quizzes and games. Review and practice again and again, game style.

  • Journal entry practice and account learning
  • Accounting quizzes by level, with scoring
  • Accounting basics that stick through repetition

You can use the app without signing in. To support sign-in, leaderboards, saved progress, and ads, we process some information: your email address (only if you sign in), nickname and learning records, usage statistics (including the install source on Android), and advertising identifiers.
